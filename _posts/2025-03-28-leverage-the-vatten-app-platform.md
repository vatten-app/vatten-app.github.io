### 1 Customer requests a service on the Vatten app

The customer opens the Vatten app and selects the service they want—like kayak,
canoe, paddleboard or umbrella rental. The customer will review the details and
place a service request

### 2 Merchant receives a service request

The request is sent instantly to a nearby merchant. The merchant reviews the details
and accepts the job if available.

### 3 Vatten app facilitates merchant and customer to meet at the pre-determined location

Once accepted, the app confirms the booking and provides both parties with
real-time location and timing details for the handoff.
